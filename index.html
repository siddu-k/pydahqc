01 // Who we are

BUILT BY STUDENTS,
FOR BUILDERS

A peer-led collective of developers, designers and innovators learning by shipping real software together.

Quantum Coders is where theory meets practice. Instead of slides alone, members learn through hackathon sprints, code labs and open-source contributions — mentored by peers who have shipped before.

From AI systems and web platforms to robotics and design, everything we run is hands-on and open to all branches.

Pydah College of Engineering Official student tech club • Pydah Group, Kakinada
